About Me

I work at Red Hat on OpenShift, Kubernetes, and large-scale agentic infrastructure.

Lately, I spend a lot of time thinking about AI-assisted development: where it helps, where it breaks down, and how to make it useful for real engineering work. I've built tools like skillsaw and various Claude plugins, mostly to make my own workflows better and more repeatable.

I also occasionally serve as a Teaching Assistant at Harvard Extension School, mostly around systems and low-level programming topics.
